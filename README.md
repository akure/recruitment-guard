# RecruitmentGuard

## Project status

**Stage:** Scope locked; implementation begins next.

RecruitmentGuard is a hackathon prototype for a **single-role candidate evidence brief**. It is designed for Priya, a recruiter at a Series B fintech startup who must prepare a hiring-manager briefing from a job description, résumé, interview transcript, and optional assessment.

## Who has this problem

Priya is closing a Backend Engineer requisition that has been open for six weeks. She needs to screen multiple candidates quickly and build an evidence-based case that a hiring manager can review.

## The bottleneck

The résumé, interview transcript, and assessment were created at different times and for different purposes. Under time pressure, a recruiter can miss material inconsistencies, such as a résumé claiming that a candidate led a team of eight while the interview describes mostly solo work. Missing or stale assessment evidence can also be silently omitted from a smooth summary.

## Why an agent

A plain summarization prompt tends to produce a coherent narrative even when sources disagree. RecruitmentGuard instead binds claims to exact source spans, extracts comparable facts into a fixed schema, checks contradictions and evidence freshness deterministically, and pauses for a human review whenever a blocking finding exists.

The system **does not make a hire/no-hire decision**. It produces an evidence brief for the recruiter and hiring manager; the human decision remains outside the system.

## Fixed sprint scope

The implementation uses one shared Backend Engineer job description and 12 synthetic candidate packets: three planted cases and nine clean controls. It contains two fair comparison paths:

| Path | Description |
|---|---|
| `baseline/` | A single-prompt summarizer with no extraction, validation, or checkpoint |
| `guarded/` | Structured extraction → deterministic validation → human checkpoint → evidence-bound brief |

The three planted cases are a team-size/ownership contradiction, a claimed-skill versus demonstrated-depth contradiction, and stale or missing assessment evidence. The primary evaluation metric is the number of planted issues surfaced out of three. Secondary metrics are guarded false positives across nine clean packets and per-packet runtime/token cost.

## Safety and data boundaries

All candidate packets are synthetic. The prototype does not connect to an ATS, email, or any external system that could take a consequential action. It produces no score, rank, or recommendation, and a flagged brief cannot be finalized until a human resolution is recorded.
