# Backend / Platform Engineer — Startup — broad ownership

## Context
Small product organization with fast iteration, ambiguous ownership, and limited specialization.

## Must-have evidence
- production API or service ownership
- Python, Go, or Java
- distributed systems fundamentals
- end-to-end delivery
- adaptability under ambiguity
- operational judgment

## Preferred evidence
- observability
- event-driven systems
- cloud cost awareness
- payments or marketplace exposure
- mentoring
- rapid iteration

## Interview focus
- What did the candidate personally own versus influence?
- Which operating trade-offs were made under incomplete information?
