# Interview transcript — Soren Pike

**Interviewer:** Tell me about the most important system you shipped recently.

**Soren Pike:** I worked on the service identity rollout at Brightwell Grid. I can walk through the failure modes, rollout plan, and what I personally changed.

**Interviewer:** What did you own directly?

**Soren Pike:** I implemented several service changes and coordinated parts of the rollout; the team shared the final ownership. Product set the customer outcome, and security reviewed the controls.

**Interviewer:** How did you handle an incident or unexpected result?

**Soren Pike:** We wrote a runbook, used dashboards to narrow the issue, and documented the decision after the incident. I would want to confirm the exact percentage before repeating the impact number.

**Interviewer:** What technologies did you use?

**Soren Pike:** Go, Terraform, and Python. The details varied by service, but I used them in production rather than only in a tutorial.
